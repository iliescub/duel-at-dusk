Duel at Dusk — Support

A four-minute western deckbuilder for iPhone.

Need help or found a bug?

Please open an issue on GitHub. Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened; a screenshot helps. We read every report.

How to play (30 seconds)

  1. Start a run and climb the Wanted ladder: three outlaws, one event, then the Dead-Eye Kid.
  2. Win the draw. Each duel starts with a timing tap: tap when the needle reaches the gold zone. Gold (Dead Eye) means you shoot first and your enemy loses a turn.
  3. Play cards. You have 5 cards and 3 energy each turn. Red Shoot cards hurt the enemy; blue Cover cards block. Press and hold any card to see what it does.
  4. Read the enemy. The panel at the top says what they will do next and how much damage you would take.
  5. Win, pick a card, repeat. Bounty you earn, win or lose, unlocks more cards in the Collection.

The same guide is in the game: tap How to Play on the home screen, or the ? button during a duel.

Frequently asked questions

There is no sound.

The game follows your iPhone's silent switch (and Silent Mode in Control Center) and the media volume. Switch silent off, raise the volume, and check Settings → Sound effects in the game.

The timing tap is too hard.

Open Settings (gear icon) and turn on Steady Hand. Every draw then counts as a normal quick draw, with no timing needed.

Can I reduce the shaking and motion?

Yes. Turn on Reduce motion in the game's Settings. The game also follows the system Reduce Motion setting.

Where is my progress saved? Can I move it to a new iPhone?

Progress is stored only on your device (the game has no accounts and no servers). It is included in a normal iPhone backup and restore. Deleting the game deletes it.

Does the game cost anything or show ads?

No ads and no accounts. See the App Store page for the current price.

Does the game use AI?

Some illustrations and audio were created with AI assistance and edited by the developer, in advance. The game itself does not use any AI service while you play.
