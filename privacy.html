Duel at Dusk — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This policy explains how the iPhone game Duel at Dusk ("the game") handles information. The short version: the game does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no network features. It works entirely offline.

What stays on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves your progress (bounty, unlocked cards, number of runs played and won, your current run) and your settings (sound, haptics, motion, Steady Hand) in the game's private storage on your device. This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the game.

Third parties

The game does not include third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software, and does not send information to any third party. Apple may collect standard diagnostic or purchase information under its own privacy policy if you have chosen to share it with developers; we do not receive personally identifying information from it.

Children

The game is rated for ages 12 and over because of its cartoon duelling theme. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Artificial intelligence

Some illustrations and audio in the game were created with AI assistance and edited by the developer. All such content is created in advance and included in the game. The game does not use any AI service while you play and does not send anything to one.

Changes

If the game ever collects any information, this policy will be updated before that change is released, and the date above will change.
